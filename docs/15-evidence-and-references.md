# Evidence and References

[Repository overview](../README.md) | [Previous: Direct answers](14-direct-answers.md) | [Evidence policy](evidence-policy.md)

This chapter records the source evidence for the review. The master table is an inventory, not a performance ranking: populations, EEG modalities, splits, and scoring units differ. It includes 60 studies and resources. The claim-evidence matrix assesses broad assertions, and the annotated bibliography identifies the original publications and their appropriate use.

**Evidence date: 30 September 2026.** [V] confirms checking against the primary source as documented during preparation; abstract-only access is identified where relevant. It does not establish independent result reproduction. `NR` and "not found" refer to inspected material. See the [evidence policy](evidence-policy.md) for confidence and access conventions.
